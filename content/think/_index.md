This page is an experiment in symbiosis between a person and a model. The intent, judgement and editing are human; the drafting, restructuring and rewording are AI. Every piece begins as a human note, comes back morphed, and is cut down until it's worth reading. Content transformation, guided by a human, morphed by AI.
